Assessment: Finite-size relational simulations show that symmetry deficit rises from a nearly permutation-invariant regime to a visibly structured regime in both mean-field-guided block ensembles and the first fully finite block-relational model. In the energy-based Monte Carlo model, symmetry deficit also develops a broad susceptibility peak whose finite-size behavior can be tested directly across multiple system sizes. The lattice Potts model adds spatial locality and provides a separate test of whether symmetry deficit sharpens in step with genuine collective ordering.
